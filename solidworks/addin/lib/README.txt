Put the SolidWorks interop assemblies here before running build.cmd:
  SolidWorks.Interop.sldworks.dll
  SolidWorks.Interop.swconst.dll
  SolidWorks.Interop.swpublished.dll
They ship with SolidWorks (C:\Program Files\SOLIDWORKS Corp\SOLIDWORKS\api\redist\) and are not part of this
repository. Use the OLDEST SolidWorks version you want to support (2016 works for 2016 and newer).
